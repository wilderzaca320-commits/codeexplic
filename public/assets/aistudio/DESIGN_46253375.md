## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-performance institutional trading terminal aesthetic engineered for precision, focus, and rapid execution. Blending cyber-fintech minimalism with an obsidian dark-room ethos, the interface channels low-light ergonomic command centers. 

The emotional signature is calculating, disciplined, and authoritative. Visual noise is stripped down to deep obsidian tones, punctuated by tactical mint/emerald bioluminescence for operational status and gains, counterbalanced by surgical crimson signals for risk and drawdown. Subtle emerald hairline borders and focused radial glow fields replace heavy shadows, producing a tactile digital instrument feel tailored for high-frequency traders, prop firm operators, and quantitative analysts.

## Colors

The palette relies on layered obsidian tiers over an ultra-deep charcoal ground to deliver deep spatial hierarchy with zero eye fatigue during extended trading sessions.

- **Primary (`#00FF87` - Neon Mint / Emerald):** Reserved for live status indicators, primary active states, peak performance metrics, active tab highlights, and luminous radar vertices.
- **Secondary (`#10B981` - Deep Mint):** Utilized for positive returns, steady gains, supporting data visualizations, and subtle border highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#EF4444` / `#F87171` - Signal Crimson):** Designates drawdown, negative P&L, stop-loss triggers, critical risk metrics, and urgent liquidation flags.
- **Obsidian Neutrals:**
  - Base Canvas: `#070908`
  - Container Tier 1 (Cards, Sidebars): `#0C100E`
  - Container Tier 2 (Inner Metrics, Recessed Inputs): `#111714`
  - Container Hover / Active Border Accent: `#16201B` / `#1C2A23`
  - Foreground Primary: `#F3F4F6`
  - Foreground Muted / Data Labels: `#8E9893`
  - Border Hairline: `rgba(0, 255, 135, 0.12)`

## Typography

Typography balances rapid UI comprehension with tabular numerical fidelity. 

- **Geist** manages headings, breadcrumbs, descriptions, and informational content. Its balanced geometric construction and clean grotesque forms ensure neutral legibility on deep obsidian backgrounds.
- **JetBrains Mono** governs all quantitative trading readouts, currency values, ratios, lot sizes, trade replay timestamps, and tabular rows. Monospaced tabular alignment guarantees numbers never jitter during high-frequency live order streaming and state updates.

## Layout & Spacing

The terminal uses a modular, fluid grid structured for dense information display while preserving scannability.

- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** Fixed left navigation rail (240px or collapsable 72px icon rail), paired with a fluid multi-column operational dashboard. Primary metric cards utilize 3-to-4 column modular matrix layouts with 20px gutters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** Sidebar collapses into an overlay menu or mini rail; metric grid flows into 2 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked workflow with horizontal swiping pill navigation strips and sticky account status headers.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Metric cards employ dense interior padding (`0.75rem` to `1.25rem`) to maximize visible telemetry above the fold without crowding touch targets.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on traditional muddy drop shadows; instead, it is achieved through calibrated surface tiers, emerald hairline borders, and localized luminescent back-glows.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure dark ground (`#070908`).
  - **Level 1 (Panels & Master Cards):** `#0C100E` with a hairline stroke: `1px solid rgba(0, 255, 135, 0.08)`.
  - **Level 2 (Nested Metric Tiles & Input Groups):** `#111714` with a hairline stroke: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
  - **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Dropdowns):** `#16201B` with `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 24px rgba(0, 255, 135, 0.08)`.
- **Neon Glow Aura:** Interactive active states (such as active range filters, radar chart nodes, and primary live indicators) emit an emerald diffusion: `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(0, 255, 135, 0.35)`.
- **Glassmorphic Accents:** Top sticky navigation headers apply `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` over `rgba(7, 9, 8, 0.82)`.

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp technical precision with softened ergonomics. 

- **Cards & Data Modules:** Standardized to `0.75rem` (`12px` to `16px`) corner radiuses, maintaining clean architectural compartmentalization.
- **Pills & Selectors:** Buttons, state indicators, filter chips, and badge components utilize full pill geometry (`9999px`) to immediately signal interactive affordance against rectangular modular grids.
- **Inner Data Bars & Sliders:** Micro-rounded corners (`4px` to `6px`) provide seamless bar fills across Win/Loss meters and Genesis score track gauges.

## Components

### Buttons & Timeframe Selectors
- **Primary Buttons:** High-contrast mint fill (`#00FF87`), dark text (`#070908`), semi-bold JetBrains Mono or Geist, flanked by an emerald aura on hover (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(0, 255, 135, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary / Filter Buttons (Pill Switches):** Enclosed inside a nested dark pill rail (`#0C100E`). Inactive items display muted grey typography (`#8E9893`); active items trigger a bright mint fill or pill highlight with crisp dark text.

### Badges, Tags & Status Indicators
- **Live Status:** Compact pill tags featuring a pulsing bioluminescent green beacon (`#00FF87` dot with a subtle ping keyframe) alongside monospaced green status copy (`Trading Open`).
- **Notification Chips:** Pill badges featuring saturated counter badges or crimson warning labels for critical alerts.

### Cards & Telemetry Tiles
- **Metric Cards:** Built on `#0C100E` containers with fine hairline border accents. Each card features an understated title paired with an informational icon tooltip (`#8E9893`), followed by high-contrast monospaced telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`). Positive values display in `#00FF87` or `#10B981`; negative values display in `#EF4444`.

### Radar Chart & Visualizations
- **Polygonal Radar:** Dark emerald-tinted radial grid lines (`rgba(0, 255, 135, 0.15)`) with an inner translucent emerald fill (`rgba(0, 255, 135, 0.12)`). Data vertices are accented with bright glowing dots (`#00FF87`) emitting subtle drop-shadow blurs.

### Progress & Gauge Trackers
- **Horizontal Progress Sliders:** Dark recessed tracks (`#111714`) paired with gradient fills transitioning from crimson (`#EF4444`) through amber (`#F59E0B`) to mint green (`#00FF87`). Markers utilize high-contrast white or mint indicator nodes.

### Inputs & Form Elements
- **Input Fields:** Dark recessed backgrounds (`#111714`) with a subtle `1px` border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`). On focus, borders transition to `#00FF87` accompanied by a soft `3px` focus ring: `rgba(0, 255, 135, 0.2)`. Placeholder copy is set in `#525D57`.
